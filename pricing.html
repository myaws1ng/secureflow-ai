Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a long-term contract? +

No. Most projects are delivered on a fixed project basis. Ongoing support plans are optional and can be customised to suit your business requirements.

How quickly can we start? +

Discovery sessions can usually be scheduled within a few business days. Project timelines depend on complexity and integration requirements.

Can you integrate with our existing software? +

In many cases, yes. During discovery we review your existing systems and recommend the most practical integration approach.

Do you provide staff training? +

Yes. Every implementation includes training and documentation to help your team confidently use the new automation.
